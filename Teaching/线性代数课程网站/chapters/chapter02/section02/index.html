Gauss消元法

线性方程组 / Gauss 消元法

例题 2.1.3 鸡兔同笼
求解 \(\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x& +& y &= & 35 && (1) \\ 2x& + & 4y& = & 94 && (2)\\ \end{array}\right.\).
解： 第一步, 方程(2)减去两倍的方程(1)得
\[\xrightarrow{{\textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{-2(1)\rightarrow(2)}}} \left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x& +& y &= & 35 && (3) \\ & & {\textcolor[rgb]{1,0,0}{2}}y& = & 24 && (4)\\ \end{array}\right.\]
第二步, 方程(4)除以\(2\)
\[\xrightarrow{{\textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{\frac12(4)}}}\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x& {\textcolor[rgb]{1,0,0}{+}}& y &= & 35 && (5) \\ & & y& = & 12 && (6)\\ \end{array}\right.\]
第三步. 方程(5)减去方程(6)
\[\xrightarrow{-(6)\rightarrow(5)}\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x& & &= & 23 && \\ & & y& = & 12 &&\\ \end{array}\right.\]
例题 2.1.4
求解 \(\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrrrr} 3x_1 & + & 2x_2 & - & x_3 & = & 6 && (1)\\ x_1 & + & 3x_2 & + & 2x_3 & = & 9 && (2)\\ 2x_1 & - & x_2 & + & 3x_3 & = & 3 && (3)\\ \end{array}\right.\).
解：交换前两个方程的顺序

\(\xrightarrow[-2(4)\rightarrow (6)]{-3(4)\rightarrow(5)}\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrr} x_1 & +3x_2 & +2x_3 & =& 9 & (7)\\ & -7x_2 & -7x_3 & =& -21 & (8)\\ & {\textcolor[rgb]{1,0,0}{-7}}x_2 & -x_3 & =& -15 & (9)\\ \end{array}\right. \)
\(\xrightarrow{-(8)\rightarrow(9)}\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrr} x_1 & +3x_2 & +2x_3 & =& 9 & (10)\\ & -7x_2 & -7x_3 & =& -21 & (11)\\ & & {\textcolor[rgb]{1,0,0}{6}}x_3 & =& 6 & (12)\\ \end{array}\right. \)

\(\xrightarrow{7(15)\rightarrow(14)}\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrr} x_1 & +3x_2 & +2x_3 & =& 9 & (16)\\ & {\textcolor[rgb]{1,0,0}{-7}}x_2 & & =& -14 & (17)\\ & & x_3 & =& 1 & (18)\\ \end{array}\right. \)
\(\xrightarrow{-\frac17(14)}\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrr} x_1 & {\textcolor[rgb]{1,0,0}{+3}}x_2 & {\textcolor[rgb]{1,0,0}{+2}}x_3 & =& 9 & (19)\\ & x_2 & & =& 2 & (20)\\ & & x_3 & =& 1 & (21)\\ \end{array}\right. \)
\(\xrightarrow[-2(21)\rightarrow (19)]{-3(20)\rightarrow (19)}\left\{\begin{array}{rr} x_1 =& 1\\ x_2 =& 2 \\ x_3 =& 1\\ \end{array}\right. \) (代回验证，成立） \(\Rightarrow\) 解集为\(\{(1,2,1)\}\).

§2.2.2

方程的三种初等变换

线性方程组 / Gauss 消元法

初等变换
记号
(1)
交换两个方程
\((i)\leftrightarrow(j)\)
(2)
某个方程乘以一个非零常数
\(\lambda(i)\)
(3)
某个方程乘一个常数加到另一个方程上
\(\lambda(i)\rightarrow (j)\)
例题 2.1.5 存在不唯一
求解 \(\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x_1 & +2x_2 & +3x_3 & +4x_4 & = & -3 & \qquad (1)\\ x_1 & +2x_2 & & -5x_4 & = & 1 & \qquad (2)\\ 2x_1 & +4x_2 & -3x_3 & -19x_4 & = & 6 & \qquad (3)\\ 3x_1 & +6x_2 & -3x_3 & -24x_4 & = & 7 & \qquad (4)\\ \end{array}\right.\)
例题 2.1.6 解存在且唯一
求解 \(\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x& +& y &= & 35 && (1) \\ 2x& + & 4y& = & 94 && (2)\\ \end{array}\right.\).
\[\Rightarrow \left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x& & &= & 23 && \\ & & y& = & 12 &&\\ \end{array}\right.\]
例题 2.1.7 解存在但不唯一
求解 \(\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x_1 & +2x_2 & +3x_3 & +4x_4 & = & -3 & \qquad (1)\\ x_1 & +2x_2 & & -5x_4 & = & 1 & \qquad (2)\\ 2x_1 & +4x_2 & -3x_3 & -19x_4 & = & 6 & \qquad (3)\\ 3x_1 & +6x_2 & -3x_3 & -24x_4 & = & 7 & \qquad (4)\\ \end{array}\right.\)
\[\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{rl} x_1 &= 1-2t_1+5t_2\\ x_2 &= t_1 \\ x_3 &= -\frac43 - 3t_2\\ x_4 &= t_2 \\ \end{array} \right. \qquad \text{其中} t_1,t_2\in \mathbb F.\]
例题 2.1.8 无解
求解 \(\left\{\begin{array}{rrrrrrr} x_1 & -x_2 & +x_3 & = & 1 & \qquad (1)\\ x_1 & -x_2 & -x_3 & = & 3 & \qquad (2)\\ 2x_1 & -2x_2 & -x_3 & = & 3 & \qquad (3)\\ \end{array}\right.\)
我们称有相同的解集的两个线性方程组互为对方的同解方程组.
定理 2.1.1
三种初等变换将线性方程组变为同解线性方程组.
证明
这里只给出第三种初等变换的证明，其他两种情形证明类似。
⚠\[ \setlength{\arraycolsep}{0.5pt} \left\{\begin{array}{ccccccccc} a_{11}x_1& + & \cdots & + & a_{1n}x_n &= & b_1\\ \vdots & & \ddots && \vdots && \vdots \\ a_{i1}x_1& + & \cdots & + & a_{in}x_n &= & b_i\\ \vdots && \ddots && \vdots && \vdots \\ a_{j1}x_1& + & \cdots & + & a_{jn}x_n &= & b_j\\ \vdots && \ddots && \vdots && \vdots \\ a_{m1}x_1& + & \cdots & + & a_{mn}x_n &= & b_m\\ \end{array}\right. \underset{-\lambda(i)\rightarrow(j)}{\overset{\lambda(i)\rightarrow(j)}{\rightleftharpoons}} \left\{\begin{array}{ccccccccc} a_{11}x_1&+ & \cdots & + & a_{1n}x_n &= & b_1\\ \vdots && \ddots && \vdots && \vdots \\ a_{i1}x_1&+ & \cdots & + & a_{in}x_n &= & b_i\\ \vdots && \ddots && \vdots && \vdots \\ (a_{j1}+\lambda a_{i1})x_1& + & \cdots & + & (a_{jn}+\lambda a_{in})x_n &= & b_j+\lambda b_i\\ \vdots && \ddots && \vdots && \vdots \\ a_{m1}x_1& + & \cdots & + & a_{mn}x_n &= & b_m\\ \end{array}\right. \]
