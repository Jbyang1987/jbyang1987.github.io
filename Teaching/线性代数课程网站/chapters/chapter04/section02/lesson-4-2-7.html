行列式 / 行列式性质

性质 4.1.1
设\(\small A= \begin{pmatrix} A_{11}&A_{12}&\cdots&A_{1k}\\ &A_{22}&\cdots&A_{2k}\\ & &\ddots&\vdots\\ & & &A_{kk}\\ \end{pmatrix}\)为分块矩阵,其中\(A_{ii}\)均为方阵.则
\[\det(A)=\prod_{i=1}^k \det(A_{ii}).\]
\[ \tiny \begin{split} \det(A) &= \sum_{(j_1,j_2,\cdots,j_n)\in S_n} (-1)^{\tau(j_1,\cdots,j_n)}a_{1j_1}a_{2j_2}\cdots a_{nj_n}\\ &=\sum_{(j_1,j_2,\cdots,j_r)\in S_r \leq r \atop(j_{r+1},\cdots,j_n)\in S_{n-r}(r+1,\cdots,n)} \left((-1)^{\tau(j_1,\cdots,j_r)}a_{1j_1}\cdots a_{r,j_r}\right) \cdot \left( (-1)^{\tau(j_{r+1},\cdots,j_n)}a_{r+1,j_{r+1}}\cdots a_{nj_n}\right)\\ &=\det(A_{11})\det(A_{22})\\ \end{split} \]
