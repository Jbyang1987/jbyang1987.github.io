矩阵(一) / 矩阵乘法的应用

我们定义如下映射
\xymatrix@R=1mm f\(\colon\) \(\mathbb\) C \ar[r]& \(\mathbb\) R^2\(\times\) 2
a+bi \ar@{|->}[r] & \(\left\)(
\begin{array}{cc}a&-b\\b&a\\\end{array}
\(\right\))
引理 3.1.3
映射\(f\)保持两边的加法和乘法. 即, 对任意 \(z_1=x_1+iy_1\), \(z_2=x_2+iy_2\)
  • \(f(z_1+z_2)=f(z_1)+f(z_2)\);
  • \(f(z_1z_2)=f(z_1)f(z_2)\).
