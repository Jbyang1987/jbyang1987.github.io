线性空间 / 一般线性空间的定义

性质 6.1.10
  1. 零向量\(\theta\)唯一;
  2. 负向量唯一;
  3. \(0\alpha =\theta\), \((-1)\alpha=-\alpha\), \(\lambda\theta=\theta\);
  4. \(\lambda\alpha = \theta\) \(\Rightarrow\) \(\lambda=0\) 或 \(\alpha=\theta\).
例题 6.1.24
  1. \({\mathbb F}^n\);
  2. \(E_n\): \(n\)元线性方程全体;
  3. \({\mathbb F}_n[x]\): \({\mathbb F}\)上次数不超过\(n\)的多项式全体;
  4. \({\mathbb F}^{m\times n}\);
  5. \({\mathbb F}={\mathbb R}\), \(V={\mathbb C}\);
  6. \({\mathbb F}={\mathbb R}\), \(V:={\mathbb R}^+:=\{r\in{\mathbb R}\mid r > 0\}\), \(r_1\oplus r_2:=r_1r_2\), \(\lambda\circ \alpha:=\alpha^\lambda\).
  7. \(C_n:=\{a_0+a_1\cos\theta+b_1\sin\theta+\cdots+a_n\cos(n\theta)+b_n\sin(n\theta)\mid a_i,b_i\in {\mathbb F}\}\);
  8. \(C[a,b]\) 区间\([a,b]\)上的连续函数全体;
