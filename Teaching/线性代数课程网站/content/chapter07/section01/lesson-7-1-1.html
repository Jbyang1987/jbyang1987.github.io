线性映射 / 线性映射

给定一个数组向量空间之间的映射\({\mathscr A}\colon {\mathbb F}^n\rightarrow {\mathbb F}^m\). 若\({\mathscr A}\)满足
\[\label{equ:linearoperator}\tag{$*$} \begin{split} {\textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{\text{(保持加法)\quad }}} &{\mathscr A}(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) = {\mathscr A}(\vec{a}) + {\mathscr A}(\vec{b});\\ {\textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{\text{(保持数乘)\quad }}} &{\mathscr A}(\lambda\vec{a}) = \lambda{\mathscr A}(\vec{a}) \end{split} \]
则\({\mathscr A}\)称为从\({\mathbb F}^n\)到\({\mathbb F}^m\)的一个线性映射.
我们有如下一一对应:
从\({\mathbb F}^n\)到\({\mathbb F}^m\)的全体线性映射 \(\underset{{\mathscr A}(\vec{x})=A\vec{x}}{\overset{\quad 1:1\quad }{\leftrightarrow}}\) \({\mathbb F}^{m\times n}\)
例题 7.1.1 伸缩变换
\(\begin{pmatrix} x\\y\\\end{pmatrix}\mapsto \begin{pmatrix} k_1x\\k_2y\\\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} k_1&\\&k_2\\\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x\\y\\\end{pmatrix}\).
例题 7.1.2 旋转变换
\(\begin{pmatrix} x\\y\\\end{pmatrix}\mapsto \begin{pmatrix} x\cos\theta-y\sin\theta\\x\sin\theta+y\cos\theta\\\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\\sin\theta&\cos\theta\\\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x\\y\\\end{pmatrix}\).
例题 7.1.3
  • \(\bullet\) \(\begin{pmatrix}1&\\&1\end{pmatrix}\) \(\leftrightarrow\) 恒等变换; \(\bullet\) \(\begin{pmatrix}0&\\&0\end{pmatrix}\) \(\leftrightarrow\) 零变换;
  • \(\bullet\) \(\begin{pmatrix}\lambda&\\&\mu\end{pmatrix}\) \(\leftrightarrow\) 伸缩变换; \(\bullet\) \(\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}\) \(\leftrightarrow\) 旋转变换;
  • \(\bullet\) \(\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}\) \(\leftrightarrow\) 反射变换; \(\bullet\) \(\begin{pmatrix}1&\\&0\end{pmatrix}\) \(\leftrightarrow\) 投射变换;
