线性映射 / 线性变换与方阵的对应

向量\(x\)和\({\mathscr A} x\)在同一组基下坐标之间的关系.
定理 7.1.2
设\({\mathscr A}\colon V\rightarrow V\)在基\(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n\)下的矩阵为\(A\). 若向量\(x\in V\)在基\(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n\)下的坐标\(X\in {\mathbb F}^n\), 向量\({\mathscr A} x\)在基\(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n\)下的坐标为 \(Y\in {\mathbb F}^n\), 则
\[Y=AX.\]
\xymatrix@C=5cm@R=2cm V \ar[r]^\(\text{基 }\)\(\alpha\)_1,\(\cdots\),\(\alpha\)_n_1:1 \ar[d]_-\(\mathscr\) A \ar@{}[dr]| \(\mathscr\) A(\(\alpha\)_1,\(\cdots\),\(\alpha\)_n) = (\(\alpha\)_1,\(\cdots\),\(\alpha\)_n)A & \(\mathbb\) F^n \ar[d]^-A
V \ar[r]^\(\text{基 }\)\(\alpha\)_1,\(\cdots\),\(\alpha\)_n_1:1 & \(\mathbb\) F^n
例题 7.1.10
记 \(\alpha_1=\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\\5\end{pmatrix}\), \(\alpha_2=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\2\end{pmatrix}\), \(\alpha_3=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}\), \(\beta_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\0\end{pmatrix}\), \(\beta_2=\begin{pmatrix}2\\4\\-1\end{pmatrix}\), \(\beta_3=\begin{pmatrix}3\\0\\5\end{pmatrix}\). 设\({\mathscr A}\)为\({\mathbb F}^3\)上线性变换满足 \({\mathscr A}(\alpha_i)=\beta_i\) (\(i=1,2,3\)). 求
  1. \({\mathscr A}\)在\(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3\)下的矩阵;
  2. \({\mathscr A}\)在自然基下的矩阵.
解答:
  1. \(A=(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)^{-1}(\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3)=\begin{pmatrix} 4&9&-5\\-10&-23&15\\-7&-16&13\\\end{pmatrix}\);
  2. \(B=(\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3)(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix} 3&-20&11\\0&-16&10\\5&-15&7\\\end{pmatrix}\).
