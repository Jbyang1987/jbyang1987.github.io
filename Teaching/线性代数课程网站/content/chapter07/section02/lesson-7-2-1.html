线性映射 / 线性映射与矩阵的对应

设\({\mathscr A}\)为从\(n\)维\({\mathbb F}\)-向量空间\(V\)到\(m\)维\({\mathbb F}\)-向量空间的一个线性变换.分别取定\(V\)和\(W\)的一组基\(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n\)和\(\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_m\). 对任意\(j=1,2,\cdots,n\), 设\({\mathscr A}(\alpha_j)\in W\) 在基\(\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_m\)下的坐标为\((a_{1j},\cdots,a_{mj})^T\), 即
\[{\mathscr A}(\alpha_j) = a_{1j}\beta_1 + \cdots + a_{mj}\beta_m.\]
则这一式子可改写为
\[{\textcolor[rgb]{0,0,1}{{\mathscr A}(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n) := \Big({\mathscr A}(\alpha_1),\cdots,{\mathscr A}(\alpha_n)\Big) = (\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_m)A}},\]
其中 \(A:=(a_{ij})_{m\times n}\in F^{m\times n}\). 称 \(A\) 为线性映射 \({\mathscr A}\) 在基 \(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n\)和\(\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_m\)下的矩阵.
例题
设\(A \in{\mathbb F}^{m\times n}\). 定义从\({\mathbb F}^n\)到\({\mathbb F}^m\)的线性变换
\[{\mathscr A}(X) = AX.\]
则\({\mathscr A}\)在自然基下的矩阵为\(A\).
证: 记\(e_1,\cdots,e_n\)为\({\mathbb F}^n\)的自然基, \(e'_1,\cdots,e'_m\)为\({\mathbb F}^m\)的自然基.则 \({\mathscr A}(e_1,\cdots,e_n):=({\mathscr A} e_1,\cdots,{\mathscr A} e_n)=(A e_1,\cdots,A e_n)=A(e_1,\cdots,e_n)=AI_n=A=I_mA=(e'_1,\cdots,e'_m)A\).
例题 （从矩阵出发构造线性映射）
分别取定\({\mathbb F}\)-线性空间\(V\)和\(W\)的一组基\(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n\)和\(\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_m\). 对于给定矩阵\(B=(b_{ij})_{m\times n}\in {\mathbb F}^{m\times n}\),如下给出一个从 \(V\)到 \(W\)的映射\({\mathscr B}\). 对任意的\(\lambda_1\alpha_1+\cdots+\lambda_n\alpha_n\in V\), 定义这一定义也可改写为\({\mathscr B}\left((\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n)\begin{pmatrix}\lambda_1\\\vdots\\\lambda_n\\\end{pmatrix}\right) = (\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_n)B\begin{pmatrix}\lambda_1\\\vdots\\\lambda_n\\\end{pmatrix}\).
\[{\mathscr B}\left(\sum_{j=1}^n\lambda_j\alpha_j\right) := \sum_{i=1}^{m} \left(\sum_{j=1}^{n} b_{ij}\lambda_j\right) \beta_i.\]
则 \({\mathscr B}\)为从 \(V\)到 \(W\)的一个线性映射,且其在基\(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n\)和\(\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_m\)下的矩阵正好为\(B\).
从而,我们得到如下一一对应:
\(\left\{\text{从$V$到$W$的}\atop \text{全体线性映射}\right\}\) \(\underset{1:1 \atop {\mathscr A}(\alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n) = (\beta_1,\cdots,\beta_n)A}{\overset{\text{基} \alpha_1,\cdots,\alpha_n \atop \text{基} \beta_1,\cdots,\beta_m }{\leftrightarrow}}\) \({\mathbb F}^{m\times n}\)
