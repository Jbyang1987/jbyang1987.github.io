例题

设矩阵\(A\)的列数和矩阵\(B\)的行数为\(n\),则
\[\operatorname{rank}(A)+\operatorname{rank}(B)-n\leq \operatorname{rank}(AB)\leq \min\Big(\operatorname{rank}(A),\operatorname{rank}(B)\Big).\]
例题
若\(A\)为\(n\)阶方阵满足\(A^2=A\),则\(\operatorname{rank}(A)+\operatorname{rank}(I-A)=n\).
例题
设\(A\in F^{m\times n}\), \(B\in F^{n\times m}\). 则
\[m+\operatorname{rank}(I_n-BA) = \operatorname{rank}\begin{pmatrix}I_m&A\\B&I_n\end{pmatrix} =n+\operatorname{rank}(I_m-AB).\]
